First release of Solfege Voxtune.
• Fix vocal pitch and timing note by note, with key and tempo detection
• Cut, join, mute, and add luk khor ornaments: flick up, flick down, shake, fall
• Advanced tools: correct pitch center, reduce drift, quantize timing, transpose, even out levels
• Record from the mic, or open WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and M4A files
• Auto-save, undo history and WAV export
