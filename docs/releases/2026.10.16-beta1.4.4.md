# Futureboard Studio 2026.10.16-beta1.4.4

Changes since **2026.10.1-beta1.4.2**. This release also carries everything
prepared for 2026.10.11-beta1.4.3, which was not published on its own.

## SoundFont Player: MIDI tracks drive the sixteen parts

The Soundfont Multi window now sets up its own MIDI tracks. Each channel
has a **+** that creates a MIDI track playing that part, named after its
preset and placed under the instrument, or puts an existing MIDI track on
the channel; **Disconnect** takes it off again. **Add MIDI Tracks** creates
one for every part that has none yet. Each change is a single undo step.

- **Mute and solo on a MIDI track now work** when it plays another track's
  instrument. Muting it silences only its notes; soloing it keeps the
  instrument audible and silences the instrument's other MIDI tracks.
  Previously mute did nothing and solo silenced the instrument entirely.
- **A hardware keyboard plays the MIDI track's channel**, the way the piano
  roll already did, so live playing and recording reach the right part.
- **A MIDI track's bank and program select its part's preset.** The part
  shows the program's preset (marked **PRG**), and picking a preset for that
  part in the browser updates the track's program, so the two always agree.
  Drum kits work on any channel this way (GM/GS channel 10, or the XG drum
  bank anywhere).
- **Fixed: variation-bank presets played the wrong sound.** A preset in
  banks 1–127 (a GS font's variation tones) played the bank 0 sound of the
  same program, in both Single and 16-channel mode.

## DAUx Direct audio backend (Windows, experimental)

A new **DAUx Direct** backend in Audio Settings streams straight to the
audio driver's kernel pins (DAUx-KS kernel streaming), bypassing the
Windows audio engine. It drives USB audio class devices with about 3 ms of
output latency at 1 ms buffers.

## Arrangement, mixer and editors

- **Smoother arrangement during playback.** The playhead and the track
  meters no longer redraw the whole arrangement on every frame, and a track
  whose volume follows automation no longer rebuilds every clip lane as its
  fader moves.
- **Faster arrangement drawing**:
  - automation lanes draw their curves in a fraction of the time and reuse
    them when nothing changed;
  - waveforms stay cached while scrolling;
  - the grid and the project length no longer walk every clip on each
    redraw.
- **Plug-in windows stay above Futureboard, not above everything.** Plug-in
  editors were "always on top" of every application; they now stay in front
  of Futureboard only, so other apps can come in front of them.
- **Mixer scrollbar**: the channel scrollbar has its own row under the strips
  and is clearly visible. Drag it or click the track to jump; it no longer
  sits faintly over the channel name plates.
- **MIDI editor scrollbars**: the piano roll's horizontal and vertical
  scrollbars can now be dragged and clicked, not only read.

## Instruments and plug-ins

- **Quick Sampler** and **Slicer**: new sample instruments with native
  editors. **Drum Sampler** now has 64 pads and multi-output routing.
- **WhiteSharp**: rebuilt as a built-in pitch corrector, with:
  - scale targeting, retune speed, humanize, flex-tune and natural vibrato;
  - Classic mode, Create Vibrato, and formant/throat control;
  - a native editor and a live pitch-correction meter.
- **Rodhareist**: native editor with a signal-path view and a factory
  preset browser, plus 29 complete, loudness-matched factory rigs. The
  Shimmer reverb no longer runs away above about 30 % shimmer.
- **WayGate**: new plug-in with native and web editors, patch routing and
  meters. **67Clipper** and **VerbSpace** DSP reworked.
- **WrapSynth**: native editor and sample clearing.
- **Built-in editors and hosting**: the built-in editors moved to native
  UI, and VST3 hosting has fixes.
- **Smoother controls**: built-in effect gains, mix and thresholds glide
  instead of stepping, and power/mode switches crossfade, so moving a knob
  no longer zips or clicks.
- **EQ-Z8**: the analyser draws with Canvas 2D where WebGL is unavailable.

## Routing

- **Plug-in MIDI output** can be routed to other tracks as their MIDI input.

## Languages

- Japanese, Thai, Chinese (Simplified) and Lao locale files are synced with
  Crowdin. The new plug-in manager strings (scan statistics, plug-in status
  and health messages) and the virtual keyboard menu item are in place for
  translation; until they are translated they show in English.

## LiveStage (separate app, not part of the Studio installer)

LiveStage is a live mixing console that runs headless on a small Linux
appliance and is operated from a web UI. It has grown from a mixer into a
full live console:

- **Channel strips**: per-strip HPF, gate, 4-band EQ, compressor and delay,
  with insert bypass fades. Above them sit DCAs, mute groups and a
  PFL/AFL/SIP monitor bus.
- **Buses and monitoring**:
  - aux, group and FX buses, mono buses, per-send pan and matrices;
  - talkback and an oscillator;
  - custom layers, Sends on Fader, and a fader-first web layout.
- **Scenes and workflow**:
  - scenes with recall scope and recall safe;
  - undo/redo, strip copy/paste and a strip library;
  - atomic saves with autosave.
- **Show-ready**:
  - users with roles and PINs, and a screen lock;
  - a personal monitor-mix page for musicians;
  - OSC and MIDI control with learn and feedback;
  - take playback for virtual soundcheck.
- **Appliance**:
  - a console setup wizard for network, Wi-Fi, audio, time zone and
    passwords;
  - real-time priority for the audio threads;
  - separate data and settings partitions, and USB storage management from
    the console and the web UI.
- **Installer**: a UEFI-bootable hybrid ISO that boots from a USB stick or a
  (virtual) CD and installs the appliance image to disk.

## Build

- **Nix (x86_64-linux)**: `flake.nix` builds every native app (Studio,
  LiveStage, JamSession, Singer and the helpers) offline from pinned
  sources; `nix develop` provides the toolchain. See `nix/README.md`.
- `xtask` limits parallel Cargo jobs by available memory and falls back to
  thin LTO on machines with less than 20 GiB free.
- The Drum Silencer neural drum-removal plug-in core is in the workspace but
  not wired into Studio yet.
