# Futureboard Studio 2026.10.1-beta1.4.2

Changes since **2026.9.27-beta1.4.1**.

## Spatial mixing and Virtual Speaker

Tracks can now be placed in a room. A spatial mix renders **binaural** for
headphones or **surround** for speakers, with each track's position, height,
spread, width and LFE, and the room's size and reflections, saved with the
project. Binaural rendering uses the **KEMAR HRTF** set with a room tail.

**Virtual Speaker** simulates listening systems — a car, a phone, a PA and
others — on the Control Room path, so you can check a mix on them without
leaving the session. It only affects monitoring; exports and recordings are
untouched.

## Master Bus visualizers

A new **Window › Visualizer** menu opens floating, always-on-top meters of the
master bus, each in its own compact window: **Spectrum**, **Stereo Image**
(a half-disc goniometer with correlation and balance meters), **Loudness**
(EBU R128 momentary, short-term, integrated, range and true peak),
**Oscilloscope** and **Spectrogram**. They are GPU-drawn and read the master
bus after the fader, before the Control Room — the signal you export.

## SoundFont Player: sixteen channels

The built-in SoundFont Player can now play **sixteen independent parts** from
one SoundFont — a General MIDI module in a track. Each MIDI channel has its own
preset, level, pan, mute and solo; route several MIDI tracks to the instrument
track, give each its own MIDI output channel, and each plays its own part. Any
channel can hold a drum kit. Choose **Soundfont Multi** in Add Track, or switch
an existing player to **16 Channels**.

The player window is redesigned: a searchable preset browser grouped the way a
General MIDI bank is laid out, the channel rack, and a three-octave keyboard
that plays the selected part. Editing a part applies live — sounding notes are
never cut to move one channel's level.

**Drum choke groups now work.** A closed hi-hat cuts the open one completely,
with a short fade instead of a click, and a stereo kit sample plays both of its
halves. Previously the second half of a stereo hit could replace the first, and
part of the choked note kept ringing.

## Built-in plug-ins

- **Imager** — new four-band stereo width processor.
- **Drum Sampler** — new 16-pad sample instrument: one-shot playback, choke
  groups, per-pad envelope, tune, gain and pan, waveform editing and sample
  management. Choosing it in Add Track now loads it into the plug-in host, so
  dropped samples play straight away.
- **Compressor** — single-band and new multiband modes.
- **Redesigned editors** for BurnLimit, 67Clipper, Transient, MixStation,
  Compressor and EQ-Z8, with a unified look across the built-in plug-ins.

## Editing and workflow

- **Piano roll** — refined layout, editor actions and controls.
- **Scoped keymaps** — shortcuts follow the focused editor; plug-in instances
  open as tabs.
- **Preferences and Browser** — redesigned navigation; the instrument list
  loads in the background.
- **Transport and mixer** — refined controls; short mixer panels keep vertical
  wheel scrolling.
- **MIDI patches** — tracks carry a bank and program selection sent to their
  instrument, per MIDI channel.
- **Rendering** — offline render improvements.

## Performance

- **Multicore rendering** and **SIMD kernels** in the engine, with better
  time-stretch latency handling.
- **MIDI edits no longer stall the UI.** MIDI schedules are built outside the
  graph lock, and unchanged clips are shared instead of rebuilt on every edit.
- Mixer meters draw from a root-level overlay, so playback redraws less of the
  window.

## Fixes

- **Network drives** — a disconnected or offline network drive no longer
  freezes the app. Network drives are probed in the background and only listed
  once they answer.
- **Warp** — legacy warp clips are repaired on load, and warp markers outside
  Warp mode are ignored.
- **Plug-ins** — better crash recovery and editor sizing.
- **Voice stealing**, **ASIO initialization** (Professional), take visibility
  on the timeline and project-loading diagnostics.
- **Linux** — visualizer windows can be dragged.
- **Plug-in database** — preset paths survive database migrations, and legacy
  entries recover their `.pst` paths.

## Project files

The project format moves from **v55** to **v58**: track bank and program
selections (v56), the spatial mix (v57) and the SoundFont Player's sixteen
parts (v58). Older projects open as before and are upgraded when saved. A
project saved by this version does not open in earlier builds, which say it
was made by a newer version.
